识别助手隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 4 日

隐私承诺

识别助手用于商品识别、建档和报价。应用不收集、出售、出租或向开发者及广告、追踪服务共享个人数据。

商品资料与照片

你录入的商品名称、规格、价格、品牌、条码、参考照片缩略图和识别特征保存在你的设备上，用于商品建档、识别和报价。应用不会将商品库或相机画面上传到开发者运营的服务器。

相机

相机用于提取商品外观特征、识别条码和读取规格文字。只有在你授权相机权限后，应用才会使用相机。

麦克风与语音识别

语音录入功能需要麦克风和语音识别权限。启用后，音频会交由 Apple 的语音识别服务转换为文字，用于填写商品名称、规格和价格；音频不会发送给识别助手开发者。

购买信息

订阅、永久买断和恢复购买由 Apple App Store 处理。应用只读取 Apple 返回的购买状态，用于解锁专业版功能；开发者不会接触你的支付信息。

数据删除

你可以在应用内删除商品档案，也可以删除应用以移除本机保存的数据。应用没有开发者运营的账号或云端商品库。

第三方服务与跟踪

应用不使用广告 SDK、分析 SDK、追踪 SDK 或开发者运营的后台服务，也不进行跨应用跟踪。

政策更新

如果未来版本改变数据处理方式，我们会在发布该版本前更新本政策及 App Store Connect 中的隐私信息。

联系我们

如有隐私问题，请通过技术支持页面提交反馈。

识别助手 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Our Privacy Commitment

识别助手 helps you recognize, catalog, and quote products. The app does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data with the developer, advertising services, or tracking services.

Product Data and Photos

Product names, specifications, prices, brands, barcodes, reference-photo thumbnails, and recognition features are stored on your device for cataloging, recognition, and quoting. The app does not upload your catalog or camera footage to a developer-operated server.

Camera

The camera is used to extract product appearance features, read barcodes, and recognize specification text. The app uses the camera only after you grant permission.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Voice entry requires microphone and speech-recognition permission. When enabled, audio is sent to Apple's speech-recognition service for transcription into product names, specifications, and prices. Audio is not sent to the 识别助手 developer.

Purchases

Subscriptions, the lifetime purchase, and restore purchases are handled by the Apple App Store. The app reads only the purchase status returned by Apple to unlock Pro features. The developer does not receive payment information.

Deletion

You can delete product records in the app or delete the app to remove data stored on your device. The app has no developer-operated account or cloud catalog.

Third-Party Services and Tracking

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and does not use a developer-operated backend service or cross-app tracking.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the Support page.
