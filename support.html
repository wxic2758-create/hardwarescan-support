识别助手技术支持

商品识别、建档与报价工具。

开始使用

首次使用时，请允许相机权限，然后拍摄商品。识别结果可以直接确认，也可以修改商品名称、规格、品牌和价格后保存到本机商品库。

常见问题

识别结果不准确怎么办？
保持商品和文字清晰、光线均匀，并让商品完整位于取景框内。可以重新拍摄，或在识别结果中修改后保存；确认后的结果会帮助后续识别。

为什么无法识别？
请检查相机权限、光线和拍摄距离。商品需要先建档，后续才能根据照片、规格或条码识别并报价。

语音录入为什么不可用？
请在系统设置中允许麦克风和语音识别权限。语音录入依赖 Apple 的语音识别服务，网络或系统语言设置可能影响结果。

我的商品资料保存在哪里？
商品资料、参考照片和识别特征保存在当前设备本地，不会上传到识别助手的服务器。

购买后没有解锁怎么办？
确认使用购买时的 Apple ID，检查网络连接，然后在应用的订阅页面选择“恢复购买”。

反馈问题

请提交支持请求，并尽量附上 iOS 版本、应用版本、操作步骤和实际结果。请不要在公开 Issue 中提交电话号码、Apple ID、支付信息或商品库照片。

识别助手 Support

Product recognition, cataloging, and quoting.

Getting Started

Grant camera permission, then photograph a product. Review the result and edit its name, specifications, brand, or price before saving it to the on-device catalog.

Common Questions

What should I do when recognition is inaccurate?
Use even lighting, keep the product and text sharp, and fit the whole product in the frame. Retake the photo or edit the result before saving. Confirmed results can improve later recognition.

Why is a product not recognized?
Check camera permission, lighting, and distance. A product must be cataloged before the app can recognize and quote it from photos, specifications, or barcodes.

Why is voice entry unavailable?
Allow microphone and speech-recognition access in Settings. Voice entry uses Apple's speech-recognition service, and network or system-language settings can affect results.

Where is my product data stored?
Product data, reference photos, and recognition features are stored locally on the current device and are not uploaded to a 识别助手 server.

What if a purchase does not unlock Pro?
Confirm the Apple ID used for the purchase, check your connection, and choose “Restore Purchases” in the subscription screen.

Contact Support

Open a support request with your iOS version, app version, steps to reproduce, and observed result. Do not include phone numbers, Apple IDs, payment details, or catalog photos in a public issue.
